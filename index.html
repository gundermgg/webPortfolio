    > Solar EVTOL Gemini V2 (2019)
    2019 Project to modify a Gemini V2 with VTOL and Solar Capability for the Ohio State Space Grant Consortium.
    Required lots of electrical/mechanical integration and a custom Pixhawk firmware build. We built this in a semester.
    I was primarily concerned with integration of electrical systems (that was most of the project's complexity), but
    also building the basic Gemini V2 kitplane and ensuring we had a flyable RC plane. I also did a bunch of work to try
    to predict the cruise/hover performance we'd get. In the aircraft's final configuration, we did a fixed-wing flight
    and a quick hover demonstration.
